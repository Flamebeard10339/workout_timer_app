An interval timer for circuit, Tabata, EMOM and any other structure you can describe
as loops inside loops.

Build a workout from named sections, each with its own duration and colour. Wrap any
group of them in a named loop with a repeat count, and nest loops inside loops as deep
as your session actually goes. Drag a row sideways to change what it sits inside.

While a workout runs, the countdown fills the screen, and the full nesting path is
stacked along the bottom in each level's own colour, so a glance tells you which round
of which block you are in. Two soft beeps at two and one second, a louder tone at zero,
and the name of each section spoken aloud as it starts.

Works entirely offline:

* No internet permission. The app cannot open a network connection.
* No accounts, no analytics, no advertising identifiers, no telemetry.
* Workouts are stored on the device and never leave it.
* Fonts are bundled, so nothing is fetched on first run.

The volume slider in the running screen scales the cues relative to your device volume,
and the screen is kept awake for as long as a workout is running.

Spoken section names use whichever text-to-speech engine your device provides. If you
want them in airplane mode, choose an offline voice under
Settings > System > Languages & input > Text-to-speech.
